Events per 100 km by route type

Urban routes see about four times the events per 100 km of motorway routes. In this dataset each vehicle runs only one route type, so vehicle and route rates can't be separated — the route differences mostly reflect where each vehicle is used, not how it's driven.

Signals, not fault findings. Detector events are investigation prompts. They do not establish driver fault or unsafe driving. Route, load, weather and time of day are not controlled here. Do not use these figures to make employment decisions about drivers.

Cleaning & coverage

Cleaning policy: dedupe on sample_id/event_id; distance ≤ 0 and fuel blank, below 0 or ≥ 10 L/interval are invalid; blank fuel is never treated as zero; L/100 km uses only intervals where both distance and fuel are valid; events with unknown trip or vehicle mismatch are excluded from vehicle and route rates.
